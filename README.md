# Mirror Shelf

Where every kid can find themselves in play.

Mirror Shelf is a curated list of dolls, figures, puzzles, books, art supplies and everyday care products that reflect many races, cultures, families and abilities. It's made for families built through transracial adoption and multicultural families, and for anyone who wants their kids to see themselves in what they play with.

**Visit the site:** https://chelseylpg.github.io/mirror-shelf/

## About

Mirror Shelf was started by Chelsey, a solo parent in Canada raising a toddler in a family built through transracial adoption. It began as a spreadsheet and grows as other families share their finds. Where possible, the list highlights makers who are Black-owned, Indigenous-owned or owned by people of the global majority, and Canadian shops.

## Corrections and suggestions

If something is tagged incorrectly, a link is broken, or you feel a product or company doesn't belong here, please open an issue on this repository and it will be reviewed.
